About PatchPulse

Preventing incidents before they happen

📍 Based in Tel Aviv, Israel

Our Mission

PatchPulse was born from a simple observation: most production incidents are preventable. We saw teams struggling with the same problems—risky deployments, missing guardrails, and lack of visibility into change impact.

Our mission is to give every engineering team the power to ship with confidence, preventing incidents before they reach production through AI-powered pre-flight analysis.

What We Do

PatchPulse analyzes your Kubernetes deployment changes before they reach production. We combine Git diff analysis with real-time cluster intelligence to provide explainable risk scores and enforce guardrails that actually make sense.

Unlike traditional policy engines, PatchPulse provides context-aware analysis. We don't just check rules—we understand your infrastructure and provide actionable insights with concrete evidence.

Our Values

Transparency: Every decision comes with explainable evidence. No black boxes.

Developer Experience: We build tools that developers actually want to use.

Security by Default: Least privilege, audit logs, and secure defaults.

Continuous Improvement: We learn from every incident prevented.

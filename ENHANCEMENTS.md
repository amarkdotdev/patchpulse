1. **Search Functionality**

   - Visual breakdown of risk scores

### 🛡️ System Enhancements

1. **5 New Guardrails (11 Total)**
   - **Privileged Security Context**: Detects privileged containers (severity: 70)
   - **Host Network**: Detects hostNetwork usage (severity: 60)
   - **Missing Readiness Probe**: Detects removed readiness probes (severity: 30)
   - **Low Replica Count**: Detects single replica in production (severity: 40)
   - Plus the original 6 guardrails

2. **Analytics API**
   - `/api/v1/analytics/summary` - Overall statistics
   - `/api/v1/analytics/risk-trend` - Risk score trends over time
   - Top guardrails triggered
   - Time-based filtering

3. **WebSocket Support**
   - Real-time decision broadcasting
   - Automatic reconnection
   - Efficient push notifications
